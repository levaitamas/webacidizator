WebAcidizator

A digital recreation of a faulty camera named the Acidizator

About

We (Kinga and Tamas) had a conceptual photo project from 2015 to 2017 that centered around a faulty camera (a Samsung NV8). The camera heavily distorted colors and forms, thus creating new context and meaning. Our goal was to present this world to others. Now, nearly a decade later, we have created a digital recreation of this camera for web browsers, allowing others to photograph this alternative world.
