WebAcidizator

An interactive recreation of a faulty camera named the Acidizator

About

We (Kinga and Tamas) had a conceptual photo project between 2015 and 2017 that centered around a faulty camera (a Samsung NV8). The camera heavily distorted colors and forms, thus creating new context and meaning. Our goal was to present this world to others. Now, nearly a decade later, we have created an interactive recreation of this camera for web browsers, allowing others to photograph this alternative world.

How to use

Point the camera, pick a frame ratio in the top bar, and press the shutter. The glitch changes on its own every few seconds, so the same scene never comes out twice. Captured photos can be saved or shared straight from the review screen.
